The office has a floor under it: the server floor, six rows of glass-doored racks with blinking machines in front, a tangle of cables behind and fans spinning on top, cold and hot aisles, coolers at every run and air handlers along the wall. The floor key in the bottom-right corner opens a list of the floors: pick one to go there (or use Page Up and Page Down), and the office is hidden while you are downstairs. The viewer remembers which floor you were on. Click a rack to swing its glass door open (front or back, whichever faces you) and see the machines bare, or click the stairwell door to shut it. The floor follows the office: the racks blink faster when agents are busy, each live session gets its own rack with its name, a light for how it is doing and a blade in its roof for every helper, and a status board by the stairs charts tool calls a minute. A failed call makes a rack flash red and smoke until the little maintenance robot rolls over to fix it, and the office cat sometimes slips downstairs for a nap on a warm rack. There is a tape library, a network core cage, a crash cart, a thermometer that warms with the day outside, and a big red emergency power off button (it only dims the lights). Click a rack for its specs, or to pull a drive.
