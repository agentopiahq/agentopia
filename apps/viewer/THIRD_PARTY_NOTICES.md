## Colorfigures, by April Zhu (MIT)

https://april-zhu.com/colorfigures

The office objects and the Claude character follow the Colorfigures drawing method: real
isometric solids shaded in three flat light/mid/dark bands by the way each face meets the
light, a heavy ink silhouette with finer inner lines, and one flat blue cast shadow. The
canvas-2D pen in `src/kit/iso.ts` is our own implementation of that method; it adapts no
Colorfigures source, but credits it as the licence asks of work built on the skill.

## Tinycharts Pro, by April Zhu
